[01] The Gaze Transfer: Whenever the WOLF locks direct line-of-sight onto an unhidden SHEEP, the Wolf status transfers to that Sheep.
[02] Hiding vs. Hunting: Taking cover blocks incoming Wolf transfers, but leaves you blind. Keeping radio promises builds AI Trust.
[03] 6 Pulses Total: When Round 6 ends, only the wearer of the Wolf collar lives—unless the optical array itself is overloaded.
